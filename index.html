About

I'm a hands-on Solutions Architect who bridges the gap between development, system integration and operational stability. My work spans end-to-end architecture — writing the initial solution design, planning releases and estimates, and seeing delivery through to the end.

Most of my career has been spent building, running and modernizing business-critical applications, largely in the telecommunications industry. I care a lot about aligning technical strategy with business goals, and I spend much of my time with stakeholders, vendors and distributed cross-functional teams.

I enjoy mentoring engineers and exploring new tools — lately, using Generative AI to speed up prototyping, legacy code analysis and documentation.
